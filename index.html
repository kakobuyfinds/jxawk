Sizing & QC checklist

  1. Save the exact variant. Record the seller, listing link, color and size. Check whether your chosen agent can open the seller link and quote that variant.
  2. Compare measurements. For shoes, check whether the chart gives foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare flat chest width and back length with an item you already wear.
  3. Review warehouse photos. If your agent supplies quality-control photos, compare the size label, color and requested measurements with your order. The directory displays listing photos.
  4. Check the return window. Confirm the seller’s exchange deadline and any return shipping charge before approving international dispatch.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

It is a directory for shortlisting products and recording details such as source links, variants, prices and fit notes. This guide uses CAFANS listings as a starting point. Confirm order eligibility with your chosen agent.

Where do the listed prices come from?

They are estimated USD item prices displayed in the CAFANS featured directory. Check the linked listing and your agent’s quote for the current variant price, exchange rate and shipping.

How can I compare two shipping quotes?

Use the same destination, packed dimensions and parcel contents for both quotes. Check which weight each carrier bills, whether tracking or insurance is included, and what charges remain payable on delivery.

Does every product have warehouse QC photos?

Photo availability depends on the agent and order. Check what photos or measurements you can request, and review your own item’s warehouse photos when available.
